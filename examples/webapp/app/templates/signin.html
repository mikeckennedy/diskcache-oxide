Where a session lives

Sessions are state, not cache, so they live in state/sessions: a plain Cache, not durable, with eviction off. If the write fails, sign-in fails with a 503 page instead of pretending to work.

The cookie is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and holds only a random session id.
